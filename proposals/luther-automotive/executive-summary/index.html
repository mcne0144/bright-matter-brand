Executive summary · Luther Automotive

One organic social standard for 50+ rooftops, run by your team.

Bright Matter proposes a six-month fractional engagement covering organic social only. It starts with a fixed-fee strategy sprint and continues with a senior lead working beside your marketing center.

Why now

Luther has the people, the brands and an in-house marketing center. Our working read is that 50+ stores post without a shared standard, so quality and risk vary by rooftop. That is a hypothesis from the outside, and the sprint tests it first.

What we do

  • Phase 1, weeks 1 to 8. Audit the group pages and a sample of 8 to 10 stores. Write the strategy, the governance playbook, content pillars and a KPI framework. Present it to leadership.
  • Phase 2, month 3 onward. Take the standard to the stores through your team. You choose an embedded lead or a lighter advisory retainer.

How success is measured

Your leadership and a sample of general managers set the KPIs during the sprint. They are signed off before Phase 2 begins, and the monthly briefing reports against them alone.
