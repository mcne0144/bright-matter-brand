# Palette

Six colors, each with one job. The discipline is what makes it read as designed:
one accent carries every call to action, and nothing else competes with it.

| Swatch | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Cream | `#FAE3B1` | Page ground |
| Cream light | `#FCEECB` | Raised surfaces: cards, steps, photo borders |
| Wine | `#7D2027` | Ink — headlines, body text, the stats band |
| Orange | `#CF5527` | **The accent.** Buttons, link underlines, section markers. Nothing else gets this color. |
| Olive | `#998731` | Supporting — service cards, small markers |
| Sky | `#5FA8C2` | Supporting — service cards, offset panels |
| Brown | `#673C34` | Soft body text, the parallax band, the footer |
| Gold | `#C9A24B` | The logo planet only |

## The mark

An open orbit ring with a planet breaking out of it — cream ring, gold planet.
`orbit-mark.png` is the transparent cutout for social avatars, decks, and print.

On the website the mark is drawn as inline SVG instead, so the ring inherits
whatever ink color sits behind it (wine on cream, cream on brown) while the
planet stays gold. Consistent identity, correct contrast on every surface.

The mark's native background is deep green `#101F1A` — use that for the video
end card and anywhere the logo needs its own field.

## Type

- **Bricolage Grotesque** — display. Headlines, 800 weight, tight tracking (-0.03em).
- **Fraunces italic** — the emphasized word inside a headline (*compound*, *matter*, *in-house*). One per headline, never more.
- **Archivo** — body and UI.
- **IBM Plex Mono** — labels, eyebrows, data.

## Motif

The punched dot, taken from the swatch cards the palette came from. It recurs
as: the logo planet, section-header markers, the circles cut into the service
cards, the numbered process dots, and the color row above the closing CTA.
One idea, repeated — that repetition is what makes it a system.
